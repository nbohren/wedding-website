Our story

Two people, many places, one day

From snowy mornings to city walks and kitchens far from home, we've been collecting places and memories together for years. On 7 May 2027 we'll gather everyone we love inside a castle above Lake Garda. Thank you for being part of the story.

Where we come from

Between the cedars and the Alps

Mona is Lebanese, Noah is Swiss — and between the two of us we speak four languages, as will many of you. Our wedding is a meeting of the Mediterranean and the mountains, of mezze and raclette, and of everyone we love in one place.
