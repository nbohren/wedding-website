Our story

Different notes, perfect harmony

Noah is a serene golfer, master of his emotions. Mona is an engineer who loves absolutely everything, except engineering (or so she says). She sings and dances; he prefers to observe, and quiet music (elevator music, according to Mona). He plays classical guitar; she loves the qanun. So many contradictions, personal and cultural, and yet they're inseparable.
